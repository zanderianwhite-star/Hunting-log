College Football

Build the ultimate all-time college football roster. Spin the slot machine, get a random program and decade, and draft one legend per round. Fill every role — from quarterback to head coach. Can your squad run the table?

How to Play

1
Spin the slot machine to get a random college program and decade.
2
Pick one player from that program's era and assign them to an open roster slot.
3
Repeat for 8 rounds to fill QB, RB, WR, DL, LB, S, K, and Coach.
4
Simulate a 15-game season. Your Strength Rating determines your record.
*
You get 1 team skip and 1 decade re-spin. Use them wisely.
